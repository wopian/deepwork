All 54 ore materials have explicit profiles. Coal and evaporites form long folded beds. Bauxite forms weathering blankets. Gold, tin and tungsten form thin branching lodes. Sulfides and cumulates form thicker linked lenses. Chalcopyrite and molybdenite form stockworks. Lithium, caesium, tantalum and beryl form zoned pegmatites. Carbonatite and kimberlite deposits use pipe forms; gemstones use smaller pockets. Length, thickness, orientation, occurrence weight and companions depend on mineral identity. Site focus increases occurrence weight; depth tiers still gate mineral availability.

Compatible minerals share coherent margins and zones, such as nickel/copper/cobalt sulfides and quartz/feldspar/mica with pegmatite ores. Each cell has one material and one mass contribution.
